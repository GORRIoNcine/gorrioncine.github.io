How we work

  1. Consultation

    You tell us the idea and deadlines. We reply within a day or two.

  2. Proposal

    We send an approach, schedule and detailed budget.

  3. Pre-production

    Script, locations, casting, permits and shooting plan.

  4. Shoot

    A light crew and daily communication with you.

  5. Delivery

    Post-production, revisions and delivery in the agreed formats.
