Feótica

Fiction · Short Film · 2026 · 13 min · Post-production

No one can escape what's inside them.

A young farmer struggles alone with his repressed desires and the guilt they cause him. Amid fantasies, dreams, and an increasingly disturbing reality, his struggle to accept his feelings leads him into a brutal confrontation with his own identity.

Adrián, a young farmer, keeps secret a desire he doesn’t even allow himself to acknowledge.

Caught between guilt, fantasy, and deep repression, he seeks refuge in the privacy of his room.

One night, a dream confronts him with what he is trying to hide from himself.

Upon waking, he returns to the corral and pushes his inner conflict to a disturbing limit.

Then, a revelation transforms the scene into a reflection of a much deeper fear: the rejection of his own identity.
