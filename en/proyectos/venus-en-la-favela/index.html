Venus en la Favela

Fiction · Short film · 2025 · 5 min · Released

What is a grain of rice?

Lucía, an architecture student living in a neighborhood of gray buildings, questions an environment that sacrifices beauty for functionality. Amid this disillusionment, a red rose becomes a small act of resistance and hope.

Lucía, a young architecture student in Cuba, wakes up every day in a neighborhood where Micro Brigada buildings dominate the landscape. One morning, as she gets ready for college, she hears on the news that another identical project is set to begin in the municipality of Habana del Este. With resignation, she turns off the TV and steps out onto the street.

As she walks toward the university, she looks at the buildings in her neighborhood with nostalgia, wondering, “Where is the beauty?” Her thoughts are interrupted when she sees wildflowers growing at the base of one of those apartment blocks—a fleeting contrast between the rigid and the organic. At the university, the conversations of her classmates—who dream of emigrating—and the strict rules of her architecture professor (who demands that they copy rationalist blueprints “without changing a single foundation”) deepen her inner conflict. At sunset, Lucía seeks refuge on the coast. As she dips her feet in the water, she hears the call of a flower vendor. She buys a red rose, a simple yet meaningful gesture that brings a smile back to her face.
