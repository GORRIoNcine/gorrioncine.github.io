Kinky

Documentary · Medium-length · 2026 · 27 min · Production

A town that learned to hear the silence of its factory.

A town and its former sugar mill, between the memory of the harvest and what is done today with its ruins.

The remaining workers walk through the mill's halls like someone visiting a relative. Their children use the yard to play baseball, and trucks on the road no longer stop. With family archive and direct sound, the documentary listens to how a town's relationship with the place that gave it its name is changing.
