About

An independent Havana production company that films close to people.

GORRIóN is an independent audiovisual production company based in Havana. Since 2018 it has produced fiction, documentary, series and music videos with small crews and a close-up look at everyday life. Its films have been shown at festivals across Latin America.

How we understand cinema

  • Looking closely

    Stories from the neighbourhood, the block and the family, filmed with patience and no postcard gloss.

  • Small crews

    Few people, lots of craft. That is how we reach places a large crew cannot fit.

  • Made with people

    Neighbours in the cast, borrowed locations, shared awards.

  • Independent cinema

    We choose what to tell and with whom. We finance through grants, co-productions and commissions.

History

  • 2018 · GORRIóN is founded

    Four friends, a borrowed camera and a rooftop in Vedado.

  • 2020 · First commissions

    Music videos and corporate films that fund our own projects.

  • 2026 · Pan de Bodega

    Shooting begins on our first series.
